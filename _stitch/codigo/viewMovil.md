## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis of artisanal specialty coffee culture and luxury Colombian eco-tourism. Rooted in the dramatic topography of Andean river canyons and mist-shrouded cloud forests, the visual narrative balances organic groundedness with editorial poise.

### Target Audience & Personality
- **Personality:** Dignified, rooted, sensory, adventurous yet tranquil, committed to terroir and heritage.
- **Audience:** Discerning specialty coffee connoisseurs, origin-conscious buyers, luxury ecotourists, and travelers seeking authentic cultural immersion in Nariño’s coffee landscape.
- **Emotional Response:** Deep connection to landscape, evocative warmth, quiet confidence, and tactile authenticity.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial Organic Luxury:** High-contrast editorial typography paired with earthy, tactile surfaces, spacious compositional margins, warm paper-tinted backgrounds, and atmospheric landscape imagery. Interface surfaces avoid artificial gloss or clinical neutrals, favoring rich botanical greens and sun-bleached mineral tones.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the coffee-growing lifecycle and terroir of the river canyon:

- **Primary (`#0F3D2E` - Bosque):** Deep Andean montane forest green. Establishes visual gravity, institutional credibility, and high-contrast brand authority. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key titles, and immersive dark containers.
- **Secondary (`#C84B3B` - Cereza):** Vibrant coffee cherry red / terracotta. Represents harvest ripeness, flavor vitality, and differentiation. Used selectively for high-intent conversion actions, seasonal highlights, badges, and sensory callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#557A46` - Hoja):** Leaf green. Denotes agricultural vitality, balance, tasting notes, and secondary UI accents.
- **Neutral (`#D9C9B1` - Arena):** Warm parchment sand. Replaces sterile cool greys to evoke tactile specialty kraft packaging, mountain soil, and warm sunlight.
- **Dark Neutral (`#1C140D` - Tostón / Dark Roast):** Roasted coffee bean tone used for deep typography, crisp line work, and contrast grounding against light backgrounds.
- **Surface Tint (`#F7F4EE` - Niebla / Pale Linen):** The foundational ambient background canvas tone providing a breathable, glare-free organic backdrop.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects editorial heritage and contemporary clarity:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys timeless craftsmanship, luxury, and regional storytelling. Used for titles, hero headers, narrative vignettes, and collection titles.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern humanist grotesque geometry delivering optimal legibility on digital screens, technical coffee roast profiles, booking dates, and pricing tables.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Headers (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.14em`) to establish crisp editorial hierarchy, honoring traditional packaging stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid structured with generous breathing room, mirroring the wide horizons of the Andean valleys:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, max-width `1440px`, outer margins `3rem` (`48px`), column gutters `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, outer margins `2rem` (`32px`), column gutters `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, outer margins `1.25rem` (`20px`), column gutters `1rem` (`16px`).

Vertical rhythm adopts an 8px base grid, expanding deliberately to `40px` (`2.5rem`) and `64px` (`4rem`) between distinct narrative chapters (e.g., origin story, coffee collection, ecotourism bookings) to maintain a serene, unhurried cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material metaphors rather than digital blurs:

- **Layered Earth Surfaces:** Depth is established by placing tinted mineral surfaces atop the light linen base (e.g., `#F7F4EE` base canvas -> `#EFE9DF` card layer -> `#0F3D2E` contrast banner).
- **Subtle Warm Shadows:** When lifting interactive cards or dropdowns, shadows use coffee roast undertones (`#1C140D` at `6%` to `10%` opacity) with wide diffusion (`blur: 16px to 32px`, `y-offset: 8px to 16px`) instead of flat achromatic black.
- **Hairline Tactile Borders:** Cards and containers use delicate 1px borders in `#D9C9B1` (40% opacity) or `#557A46` (20% opacity) to evoke artisanal card stock and linen edge stitching.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry:
- Standard elements (buttons, inputs, chips) feature `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radii.
- Media tiles and product cards utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Larger containers and modal sheets adopt `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`).

This restrained curve language prevents the interface from feeling plasticky or overly toy-like, honoring the angular edges of mountain ranges, river stones, and traditional coffee sack corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F3D2E` (Bosque), text `#F7F4EE`, 1px subtle tone border. Hover state transitions smoothly to `#1A5642` with a subtle `2px` upward translation.
- **Secondary (Cereza Accent):** Background `#C84B3B`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved for primary checkout, tour reservations, and limited edition drops.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#0F3D2E` (or `#D9C9B1` on dark surfaces), text in matching border color.
- **Padding & Typography:** `12px 24px`, font `label-lg`, uppercase letter spacing on high-level actions.

### Chips & Origin Tags
- Used for roast profiles, elevation markers (e.g., "1.850 msnm"), and tasting notes (e.g., "Panela", "Cítrico", "Frutos Rojos").
- Background `#EFE9DF` with 1px border `#D9C9B1`; text `#0F3D2E` in `label-caps`. Active or selected chips switch to `#0F3D2E` fill with light text.

### Cards (Product & Experience)
- **Coffee Bag Display:** Vertical card with `#F7F4EE` background, 1px soft border `#D9C9B1`. Upper 65% showcases packaging photograph; lower 35% displays origin name in Playfair Display, tasting notes chip-row, and pricing.
- **Ecotourism Experience Card:** Landscape ratio with full-bleed Andean landscape photo, overlaid with a bottom-weighted gradient transitioning into `#0F3D2E` containing stay duration, elevation, and direct booking trigger.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F6`. Default border 1px `#D9C9B1`. Focus state transitions border to `#0F3D2E` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 61, 46, 0.12)`). Label typography rests in `label-caps` directly above the field.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`4px` radius) or circle controls. Border 1.5px `#557A46`. Checked state fills `#0F3D2E` with a sand/cream checkmark icon.

### Terroir Spec Sheets & Flavor Wheels
- Tabular data striping alternating between transparent and `rgba(217, 201, 177, 0.25)`.
- Iconography for *Grano*, *Río*, *Territorio*, and *Fruta* rendered in single-weight botanical line style using `#0F3D2E`.
